## P1's answers to the schema questions (review of 18 October, approved by the user)

No schema change before submission; the pack models the demo scenario exactly and leaves everything else absent.

1. Product approvals: absent when cost or time is unknown; NONE for the stainless-steel tumbler only with Food Act / Food Regulations text (done, see above).
2. Marketplace fees: absent; no invented percentages.
3. Indirect tax: rate = the tumbler line's rate; threshold RM500,000 with "manufacturers register; a reseller pays sales tax on import"; `taxBase` from the Sales Tax Act valuation section; Form E with the tariff line and 40% RVC (all done).
4. E-invoicing: keep the RM3M scalar (strictly less than); other conditions stay in the ledger note.
5. Ledger sidecar: stays in `docs/compliance`; the verifier does not read it yet, so it must be checkable by a person from the repo.
