## 1. A money word up to 12 characters before a phone or card number vetoes every pattern of its recognizer, including the +65/+60 and 01X patterns, so some phone and card numbers reach chat_messages and the model provider unredacted

- **Fingerprint:** `packages/privacy/src/detect.ts:candidatesOf:negative-context-veto-on-every-pattern`
- **Verifier:** `v-5` (independent, source-only)

### Claimed root cause

packages/privacy/src/detect.ts candidatesOf (lines 105-114): for every pattern p of a recognizer, if r.negative is set, a match is skipped when neg.within(start - negativeBefore, start) finds any whole veto word inside the 12 characters before it (negative_before: 12, data/pii/recognizers.json:16). packages/privacy/src/config.ts:143 compiles hogee_negative_context once per recognizer, and the pattern schema (config.ts:36-42) has no per-pattern negative flag. data/pii/recognizers.json puts the money-word list (budget, fee, total, s$, $, rm, ...) on SgPhoneRecognizer (311), MyPhoneRecognizer (385) and CreditCardRecognizer (168). So the veto also suppresses the context-free +65/+60/01X phone patterns and the grouped card patterns, beyond the 'word just before the match' the $comment (recognizers.json:7) specifies. Nothing downstream catches the miss, because data/pii/canary-patterns.json covers only national and tax IDs.

### Description

POST /api/chat runs as the founder persona, which is anonymous by design. The message (ChatBody, at most 4000 characters) goes through @hogee/privacy redact() via redactOrFail. The result becomes the last planner user turn, and after the turn it is stored in chat_messages as the user turn. The guard runs the same redact() on every user text part of each planner prompt, then runs assertNoIds over the serialised prompt. Three recognizers carry one hogee_negative_context word list at recognizer level: SgPhoneRecognizer, MyPhoneRecognizer and CreditCardRecognizer (also IdPhoneRecognizer and MyBankAccountRecognizer). The list includes budget, fee, total, cap, price, rm, sgd, s$ and $. config.ts compiles it into one regex per recognizer. detect.ts candidatesOf applies it inside the per-pattern loop, so it covers patterns that need no context and cannot be amounts: '+65 9999 9999', '+60 ...' and the MY '01X' mobile pattern, as well as the grouped card patterns. A match is dropped when any whole veto word lies anywhere in the 12 characters before it, but the file's $comment says only 'a word just before the match' should veto. No other recognizer catches the dropped number. The canary is by design limited to MY_NRIC, ID_NIK, ID_NPWP and SG_NRIC, so the call is not blocked. Hand evaluation of the bundled regexes and Ranges.within (no code run) gives these inputs back unchanged from redact(), with assertNoIds not throwing: 'Budget S$8k, HP +6591234567'; 'Fee $5, HP +65 9123 4567'; 'Budget: +65 9123 4567'; 'Bajet RM 10k, tel 012-345 6789'; 'Bajet RM 10k, tel +60 12-345 6789'; 'Fee $5, card 4111 1111 1111 1111'. The NIK 4-4-4-4 pattern does not catch the card because validIdProvince rejects 41. The bank-account patterns share the same veto and need context. The number is then stored, sent to the configured provider (up to 6 planner steps per turn) and re-sent with the history on every later turn. Correction to the hunter: two of its payloads do not leak by source. 'Budget S$8k, HP +65 9123 4567' and 'My budget, tel +65 9123 4567' give '... +65 <SG_PHONE>'. The context-required bare 8-digit SG pattern starts 4 characters later, so its own 12-character veto window misses the money word, and 'HP'/'tel' supplies its context. So a space-separated +65 number with a phone word nearby leaks in full only when the veto word is within about 8 characters before '+65'. Without a phone word, or written without a space ('+6591234567', where the bare pattern cannot start), the 12-character veto alone suppresses it. MY 01X and +60 numbers and grouped card numbers have no such fallback. Effect: a privacy control on the in-scope founder-text-to-model-provider boundary fails open for ordinary budget chat. The founder's (or a third party's) phone or card number is disclosed to the third-party model provider and persisted in chat history, contrary to the redact-before-store/send design. No attacker gains authority. Smallest fix: make the veto per pattern. Add a hogee_negative_context flag or list on the pattern, off for the +65/0065/(65), +60 and 01X patterns. Veto only when the word is directly before the match (only spaces, or a glued currency symbol, between them), as the $comment states.

### Evidence

- `data/pii/recognizers.json:7`: The $comment defines hogee_negative_context as 'a word just before the match vetoes it', but context_window.negative_before is 12 (line 16), and detect.ts vetoes on any whole veto word anywhere in that window.
- `packages/privacy/src/detect.ts:111`: 'if (r.negative)' sits inside 'for (const p of r.patterns)' (line 105), so the veto applies to context-free country-code patterns as well as to the context-required bare patterns it was written for.
- `packages/privacy/src/config.ts:121`: wordRegex: a letter-ending veto word must be a whole word ('RM 10k' vetoes, 'RM10k' does not). Symbol-ending words ('$', 's$') match whenever not preceded by a letter or digit, so a '$5' up to 12 characters earlier vetoes.
- `data/pii/recognizers.json:279`: The bare SG pattern is context-required, and its lookbehind excludes a preceding digit, '+' or '-'. It cannot start inside '+6591234567', so that form has no fallback. For '+65 9123 4567' it starts 4 characters after '+65', so its own veto window is shifted. That is why 'Budget S$8k, HP +65 9123 4567' and 'My budget, tel +65 9123 4567' are still redacted to '... +65 <SG_PHONE>' (correcting the hunter's payloads), while 'Fee $5, HP +65 9123 4567' and 'Budget: +65 9123 4567' are not.
- `data/pii/recognizers.json:348`: The MY '01X-XXX XXXX' mobile regex (score 0.6, no context required) shares MyPhoneRecognizer's list ('rm' line 386). The landline pattern (line 353) needs 0[3-9], and the bank-account patterns (lines 200, 206) are context-required and carry the same veto. So nothing re-catches '012-345 6789' after 'RM 10k, tel '.
- `packages/privacy/src/validators.ts:50`: validIdProvince rejects 41-49, so the NIK 4-4-4-4 pattern (recognizers.json:77; canary-patterns.json:47) never matches a Visa-style '4xxx xxxx xxxx xxxx'. A vetoed card is therefore neither redacted nor caught by the canary.
- `data/pii/canary-patterns.json:3`: By design the canary checks only national and tax ID shapes. The last entity is SG_NRIC (line 58), and there is no phone, card or bank pattern, so a redaction miss for those entities is never blocked outbound.
- `packages/privacy/test/entities.test.ts:131`: The veto tests ('card total $4111111111111111', 'visa S$4111111111111111', 'call me, budget 91234567') cover only glued or adjacent amount-shaped numbers and the bare SG pattern. No test puts a veto word within 12 characters of a +65/+60/01X number or a grouped card. The +65 test at line 127 uses a non-veto word ('Buddy').
- `packages/agent/src/planner/planner.ts:150`: The last user turn's text is sent as its own text part next to the context block, so the provider receives exactly the redactor's output for the founder's message.
